# patearn — 14 Pattern Definitions
## Full sub-signal criteria, source checks, and scoring rubric

Pattern weights: W9 (highest) through W5 (lowest). MAX contribution per pattern = W × 3 signals × 2 (Yes) = W×6.

---

### PATTERN 1 — ROCE / ROE Trajectory (W9 · Quality Gate)
*Discriminative lift: 4.6× — appears in 82% of confirmed multi-baggers vs 18% of controls. Highest signal in the framework.*

**r1 — ROCE currently > 18%**
- Yes: ROCE ≥ 18% in the most recent full year. Source: Screener.in ratio table.
- Partial: ROCE 14–18%, or current year is COVID/one-time impacted but 3-year average ≥ 18%.
- No: ROCE < 14%.

**r2 — ROCE rising for 3+ consecutive years**
- Yes: 3 consecutive annual increases in ROCE with no interruption. Source: Screener 10Y data.
- Partial: 2 consecutive increases, OR 3 years with one year flat (not declining). COVID year distortions may be excluded if documented.
- No: ROCE declining or flat for 2+ years.

**r3 — ROCE improving faster than sector average**
- Yes: Company ROCE improvement rate > sector peer median over 3 years. Source: Screener peer comparison.
- Partial: Peer comparison unavailable, but ROCE improvement is strong in absolute terms (> 500 bps over 3 years).
- No: Sector is improving faster than the company.

**Double-count warning:** If both Pattern 1 (ROCE) and Pattern 2 (Operating Leverage) are scoring maximum, note that both are measuring capital efficiency from different angles. The score may be concentrating strength in one underlying factor.

---

### PATTERN 2 — Operating Leverage (W9 · Quality Gate)
*EPS growing faster than revenue = fixed cost leverage and/or mix improvement — the engine of re-rating.*

**o1 — 5Y EPS CAGR > 2× 5Y Revenue CAGR**
- Yes: EPS CAGR over 5 years is ≥ 2× revenue CAGR over 5 years. Source: Screener 10Y data.
- Partial: EPS CAGR is 1.5–2× revenue CAGR, OR 3Y window shows the relationship clearly even if 5Y is distorted.
- No: EPS CAGR < 1.5× revenue CAGR.

**o2 — EBITDA margin making higher highs each year**
- Yes: OPM in most recent year is higher than all previous 3 years. Source: Screener quarterly data or P&L.
- Partial: OPM trending up over 3 years with one year of consolidation.
- No: OPM declining or flat over 3 years.

**o3 — PAT margin expanded in each of last 3 years**
- Yes: PAT/Revenue ratio increased in each of FY(n), FY(n-1), FY(n-2). Source: Screener annual data.
- Partial: 2 of 3 years expansion, OR current year expansion is strong and the miss was a documented one-off.
- No: PAT margin flat or declining.

### PATTERN 3 — Structural Sectoral Tailwind (W8 · Quality Gate)
*Not a narrative — a named, funded, time-bounded policy or structural shift that creates non-discretionary demand.*

**s1 — Named government scheme or PLI with specific budget outlay**
- Yes: A specific scheme exists (e.g. RDSS ₹3.03 lakh Cr, PLI electronics ₹41,000 Cr, PM Gati Shakti) with announced outlay and timeline. Source: Ministry website, budget documents.
- Partial: Sector beneficiary of capex cycle without a named scheme, OR scheme exists but outlay is small (< ₹10,000 Cr for the sector).
- No: No specific policy tailwind; company dependent on organic private demand only.

**s2 — Company sits at the bottleneck of the theme**
- Yes: The company's product is a non-substitutable input to the sector's growth (e.g. conductor = no T&D expansion without it). Competition cannot easily enter due to certifications, approvals, or capital requirements.
- Partial: Company is a significant but not sole supplier in the theme; 2–3 other qualified competitors exist.
- No: Company is one of many interchangeable suppliers; no differentiation at the input level.

**s3 — Tailwind has 5+ years still to run**
- Yes: Policy or structural demand driver is explicitly multi-year (e.g. NEP transmission targets through 2032, defence procurement pipeline through 2030). Source: policy documents.
- Partial: Tailwind is real but timeline is 2–4 years, or uncertain beyond current budget cycle.
- No: Tailwind is near its peak or less than 2 years remaining.

### PATTERN 4 — Valuation Asymmetry (W8 · Quality Gate)
*The market is pricing a temporarily cyclical business as permanently impaired.*

**v1 — PE below 15× on mid-cycle EPS, OR below 80% of own 5-year median PE**
- Yes: Current PE < 15× (using normalised/mid-cycle EPS if current year is distorted), OR current PE < 80% of own 5-year median. Source: Screener PE history.
- Partial: Current PE 15–20×, OR current PE 80–95% of own 5-year median.
- No: PE > 20× or above own historical median.

**v2 — P/B below 1.5×, OR discount to sector median despite equal/higher ROCE**
- Yes: P/B < 1.5×, OR company ROCE ≥ sector median but P/B is 20%+ below sector median.
- Partial: P/B 1.5–2.5×, or slight ROCE premium with minor valuation discount.
- No: P/B > 2.5× or no valuation discount to sector.

**v3 — EV/EBITDA below 8×, OR discount to sector despite superior margins**
- Yes: EV/EBITDA < 8×, OR company OPM ≥ sector median but EV/EBITDA is 20%+ below sector median.
- Partial: EV/EBITDA 8–12×, or marginal discount to sector.
- No: EV/EBITDA > 12× or at premium to sector.

### PATTERN 5 — Balance Sheet Deleveraging (W8 · Quality Gate)
*Debt coming down while the business expands = capital efficiency compounding.*

**b1 — Debt/Equity falling over last 3 years**
- Yes: Net D/E declined in each of the last 3 years. Source: Screener balance sheet data.
- Partial: D/E declined in 2 of 3 years, or was stable (not rising) with absolute debt reduction.
- No: D/E rising.

**b2 — Interest coverage > 5× and rising**
- Yes: EBIT/Interest > 5× in most recent year AND has risen over 3 years. Source: Screener ratio table.
- Partial: Coverage 3–5× and rising, OR > 5× but flat.
- No: Coverage < 3× or declining.

**b3 — FCF positive in 2 of last 3 years**
- Yes: CFO - Capex > 0 in 2 of the last 3 annual periods. Source: Screener cash flow data.
- Partial: FCF positive in 1 of 3 years but trend improving.
- No: FCF persistently negative.

### PATTERN 6 — Product / Revenue Mix Premiumisation (W7)
*The business is becoming higher-value without adding proportional cost.*

**m1 — Premium product share growing for 3+ years**
- Yes: Named premium segment (HTLS conductors, specialty API, CPVC over PVC, branded innerwear, etc.) has grown as a % of total revenue for 3+ consecutive years. Source: Annual Report segment notes, investor presentations.
- Partial: 2 years of documented growth, or product mix improving but not clearly separated in disclosures.
- No: No premium segment identified, or mix declining.

**m2 — Segment margins of value-added products are expanding**
- Yes: The premium segment's EBIT margin has improved over 3 years and is above the company's blended OPM. Source: Segment P&L in Annual Report.
- Partial: Margin data not separately disclosed but MD&A references premium product profitability improvement.
- No: No margin data for premium segment, or premium segment margin is below blended margin.

**m3 — Evidence in annual reports and investor presentations**
- Yes: MD&A or investor presentation explicitly discusses premium mix strategy with quantified targets, product names, and geographic expansion plans.
- Partial: Strategy mentioned but without quantification.
- No: No reference to mix premiumisation in disclosed documents.

---

### PATTERN 7 — Capex Cycle Completion (W7)
*Heavy investment is over; free cash flow is now being generated and compounded.*

**c1 — Capex as % of revenue declining for 2+ years**
- Yes: Capex/Revenue declined in each of the last 2 years and is now < 3% of revenue. Source: Cash flow statement / Screener.
- Partial: Capex/Revenue declining but still > 3%, or declined in 1 of 2 years.
- No: Capex/Revenue rising or > 5%.

**c2 — No large debt-funded greenfield capex in pipeline**
- Yes: Management has explicitly guided for maintenance/debottlenecking capex only, no greenfield. Confirmed in concalls. Capex guidance < 2% of revenue.
- Partial: Brownfield expansion discussed but modest (< 15% of existing asset base).
- No: Greenfield or large acquisition announced or in progress.

**c3 — Asset turnover (Revenue/Total Assets) improving**
- Yes: Revenue/Total Assets ratio improving over 3 years. Source: Screener.
- Partial: Flat with one-year improvement.
- No: Asset turnover declining.

### PATTERN 8 — Promoter / Insider Accumulation (W7)
*Insiders are buying with their own capital during the period when institutions are selling.*

**p1 — Promoter holding risen 100+ bps in last 3 years**
- Yes: Promoter % increased ≥ 100 bps from 3 years ago to today. Source: BSE quarterly shareholding archive.
- Partial: 50–100 bps increase, or increase in 2 of 3 years.
- No: Promoter holding flat or declining.

**p2 — Zero or minimal pledge on promoter shares**
- Yes: Promoter pledge = 0% in all recent filings. Source: BSE shareholding pattern.
- Partial: Pledge < 5% and declining.
- No: Pledge > 5%, especially if not declining.

**p3 — No equity dilution in last 5 years**
- Yes: Share count stable or reduced (buybacks) over 5 years. No QIP, rights issue, or significant ESOP dilution. Source: Screener share count history.
- Partial: Minor ESOP dilution (< 2% of shares outstanding over 5 years).
- No: QIP, rights issue, or meaningful dilution in last 5 years.

**Double-count warning:** Pattern 8 (Promoter Accumulation) and Pattern 10 (Institutional Neglect) both measure the ownership landscape and may reflect the same underlying reality — the FII capitulation that allows promoter accumulation. If both are scoring Yes, acknowledge that one factor is driving both signals.

### PATTERN 9 — Export / Global Revenue Expansion (W6)
*The domestic market story is already happening; global demand is not yet priced in.*

**e1 — Export revenue growing faster than domestic for 3+ years**
- Yes: Export CAGR > Domestic CAGR over 3 years, confirmed in segment disclosures. Source: Annual Report geographic segment note, investor presentations.
- Partial: Export share growing in 2 of 3 years, or total export revenue growing but domestic also growing strongly.
- No: Export revenue flat or declining.

**e2 — Export share > 25% or growing toward that level**
- Yes: Exports > 25% of segment/company revenue.
- Partial: Exports 15–25% and clearly growing.
- No: Exports < 15% with no clear growth trajectory.

**e3 — Exports to premium markets (US / EU / Middle East / Australia)**
- Yes: Named customers or destinations in US utility sector, EU, UAE, Australia. These are high-certification, high-margin, repeat-order markets. Source: Investor presentations, Annual Report.
- Partial: Exports to Asia/Africa only (lower pricing power), or destinations not disclosed.
- No: No export disclosure or only regional low-margin markets.

### PATTERN 10 — Institutional Neglect (W6)
*The opportunity window exists because sophisticated capital has left. When they return, the re-rating begins.*

**i1 — FII holding at 3–5 year low**
- Yes: FII % is at its lowest in 3–5 years. Source: BSE quarterly shareholding archive, Trendlyne.
- Partial: FII holding declining but not yet at multi-year low.
- No: FII holding stable or rising.

**i2 — Fewer than 5 analyst reports in last 12 months**
- Yes: < 5 active brokerage notes or initiation reports in last 12 months. Source: Trendlyne, BSE filings, broker sites.
- Partial: 5–10 reports, limited institutional coverage.
- No: > 10 active analyst reports or recent institutional initiation.

**i3 — Retail shareholder count < 50,000**
- Yes: Total public shareholders < 50,000. Signals limited retail awareness.
- Partial: 50,000–100,000 shareholders.
- No: > 100,000 shareholders or high retail ownership footprint.

### PATTERN 11 — Tight Base & Breakout Trigger / VCP (W6 · Technical · ⚠ Chart Check Required)
*Volatility contraction before an expansion move — the technical signature of institutional accumulation.*

**ALL SIGNALS IN THIS PATTERN ARE ESTIMATED UNLESS ATR/VOLUME DATA HAS BEEN PULLED.**

**t1 — 20-day tight base, positioned near 52-week high**
- Yes: (Max High 20D - Min Low 20D) / Min Low 20D ≤ 8%, close in top 20% of the 20-day range, stock within 25% of 52-week high. Drawdown depth ≤ min(15%, 35% of prior advance). Prior base duration ≥ 12 months = Yes.
- Partial: Range 8–12%, top 30% of range, within 30% of 52W high. Prior base duration 4–12 months.
- No: Stock more than 30% below 52W high, or range > 12%.

**t2 — Volatility contraction confirmed**
- Yes: ATR(10)/ATR(50) ≤ 0.6 AND average daily candle range last 10 days ≤ 70% of previous 10 days. Both conditions met.
- Partial: One of the two conditions met.
- No: ATR ratio > 0.8, volatility expanding.

**t3 — Volume dry-up with breakout confirmation**
- Yes: Average volume during base ≤ 70% of prior 20-day average. Breakout: close > base high by ≥ 1%, volume on breakout day ≥ 1.8× base average, AND close in upper 50% of breakout day's range.
- Partial: Volume dry-up confirmed but breakout volume only 1.3–1.8×, or close in lower 50% of day range.
- No: No volume dry-up or no clean breakout.

**Note on prior base duration:** The 18-month base is a quality amplifier, not a gate. A stock with a 6-month base that is technically perfect still scores Partial on t1 but is not excluded. The shorter base reduces the score slightly — it does not disqualify.

### PATTERN 12 — Working Capital Efficiency (W5)
*Negative working capital means the business is self-financing — a sign of pricing power over both customers and suppliers.*

**w1 — Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC) below 30 days, ideally negative**
- Yes: CCC = (Debtor Days + Inventory Days) - Creditor Days < 30 days, or negative. Source: Screener ratio table.
- Partial: CCC 30–60 days, or historically negative but slightly elevated this year.
- No: CCC > 60 days and worsening.

**w2 — Receivable days stable or improving over 3 years**
- Yes: Debtor Days declining or flat over 3 years. No year-on-year increase > 10 days. Source: Screener.
- Partial: One year of increase but trend otherwise flat.
- No: Receivable days rising consistently (> 15 days over 3 years = Hard Disqualifier proximity alert).

**w3 — Payable days ≥ Inventory days + Receivable days**
- Yes: Creditor Days ≥ (Debtor Days + Inventory Days). Suppliers are financing the business.
- Partial: Creditor Days within 20% of (Debtor + Inventory).
- No: Creditor Days < Debtor + Inventory, meaning the company is net financing its supply chain.

### PATTERN 13 — Management Quality and Disclosure (W5)
*The quality of what management says and whether the numbers confirm it.*

**g1 — Concall guidance vs delivery: < 15% miss rate**
- Yes: Across last 8 quarters, management's guidance on revenue/EBITDA was missed by < 15% in fewer than 2 quarters. Source: Trendlyne concall transcripts.
- Partial: 2–3 misses in 8 quarters, or guidance is consistently conservative (underguides and over-delivers).
- No: > 3 guidance misses > 15% in last 8 quarters.

**g2 — Segment-level data disclosed consistently**
- Yes: Each segment's revenue and EBIT margin disclosed separately in each quarterly result and Annual Report. Not aggregated.
- Partial: Segment revenue disclosed but margins not separately, or inconsistent across years.
- No: Company reports single-segment or no segment data despite operating multiple businesses.

**g3 — No salary hike for promoter-directors while PAT was flat or falling**
- Yes: Promoter-director remuneration did not increase during the years PAT was flat or declining. Source: Annual Report → Board's Report → Remuneration disclosure.
- Partial: Modest increase (< 15%) during weak PAT years.
- No: Significant promoter salary increase while company underperformed.

### PATTERN 14 — Relative Strength and Stage of Move (W6 · Technical · ⚠ Chart Check)
*Institutional positioning relative to the index, and where we are in the price cycle.*

**ALL SIGNALS IN THIS PATTERN ARE ESTIMATED UNLESS RS LINE DATA HAS BEEN PULLED.**

**rs1 — RS line vs Nifty trending up for 3+ months before price breakout**
- Yes: Relative Strength line (stock price / Nifty index) has been making higher highs for 3+ months even before the price has broken out. Source: Chartink, TradingView RS comparison.
- Partial: RS line flat-to-slightly-positive for 1–2 months.
- No: RS line declining — stock underperforming the index.

**rs2 — Stock holds better than index during corrections**
- Yes: In the most recent 5%+ market correction, the stock fell less than 70% of the index decline (e.g. if Nifty fell 8%, stock fell < 5.6%).
- Partial: Stock fell 70–100% of index decline.
- No: Stock fell more than the index — relative weakness confirmed.

**rs3 — First or second base in the price cycle, not a third+ after a large run**
- Yes: This is the first or second VCP/consolidation after a period of flat or neglected price action. The stock has not previously done a 100%+ run from a prior breakout in the same cycle.
- Partial: Second base but stock already did a 50–100% move from prior breakout.
- No: Third or later base, stock already up > 200% from prior breakout — late in the cycle.

**Double-count warning:** rs1 (RS line trending) and i1 (FII at multi-year low) frequently co-occur in the same setup — FII capitulation produces RS underperformance, and FII return produces RS outperformance. Both are partly measuring the same institutional dynamic.

## IMPLEMENTATION MAPPING (scoring.py)

The automated Stage-1 scorer [`src/automation/scoring.py`](../../src/automation/scoring.py) implements this
methodology **as far as the fundamentals feed allows**. Two things a future reader must know:

1. **Quality-Gate patterns 1–5 are implemented faithfully** — same numbers, same weights (ROCE 9 ·
   Operating Leverage 9 · Sectoral Tailwind 8 · Valuation 8 · Balance Sheet 8). This is the load-bearing screen.
2. **Patterns 6–14 in the code are a DELIBERATE reorganization to what is numerically computable.** The
   code's pattern *numbers, labels and weights for 6–14 do NOT line up 1:1* with this document's 6–14 —
   do not assume "pattern #N" means the same thing in both places. Use the map below.

| code # (`scoring.py`) | code label | what it computes | methodology # (this doc) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ROCE | ROCE level + real 3Y trend/avg | **1** ROCE / ROE Trajectory |
| 2 | Operating Leverage | profit-vs-sales growth, OPM + 3Y trend | **2** Operating Leverage |
| 3 | Structural Sectoral Tailwind | *(Phase-4 judgment; scored Partial)* | **3** Structural Sectoral Tailwind |
| 4 | Valuation | PE, PB | **4** Valuation Asymmetry |
| 5 | Balance Sheet Quality | D/E, interest coverage | **5** Balance Sheet Deleveraging |
| 6 | Promoter Conviction | promoter holding, pledge, accumulation | **8** Promoter / Insider Accumulation |
| 7 | Export / Mix Inflection | *(Phase-4 concall/segment; Partial)* | **6** Mix Premiumisation + **9** Export |
| 8 | Institutional Neglect | FII holding (lower = better) | **10** Institutional Neglect |
| 9 | Earnings Momentum | profit growth TTM/3Y + acceleration | *computable adjunct — no distinct methodology pattern* |
| 10 | Margin Expansion | OPM level + 3Y OPM trend | *margin facet of methodology #2 / #6* |
| 11 | VCP / Technical | *(Phase-4 bhav + ATR; Partial)* | **11** Tight Base & Breakout / VCP |
| 12 | Receivables Discipline | debtor days | **12** Working Capital Efficiency · receivables (w2) |
| 13 | Working Capital | cash-conversion cycle, WC days | **12** Working Capital Efficiency · CCC (w1) |
| 14 | Volume Confirmation | *(Phase-4 bhav; Partial)* | volume facet of **11** / **14** |

**Methodology patterns with no distinct code implementation** (Phase-4 qualitative only): **#7 Capex Cycle
Completion**, **#13 Management Quality & Disclosure**, **#14 Relative Strength & Stage of Move** (RS itself
is computed in the RS suite, not the patearn scorer).

**Weights.** The code's 6–14 weights were adapted to this reorganization and differ from the nominal weights
above where the semantics map (e.g. VCP is W5 in code vs W6 here). **This is intentional. Changing
`scoring.py`'s weights to match this document is a scoring-behaviour change — every NS moves — a signed
decision (D114), not a doc edit.** This document remains the canonical *methodology*; `scoring.py` is its
*computable Stage-1 approximation*.
